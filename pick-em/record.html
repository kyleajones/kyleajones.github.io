How to Play

Welcome to 3woks NFL Pick'em! Make your picks each week to win it all.

Scoring Rules

  • Correct Pick: 3 Points
  • Push / Tie: 1 Point
  • Incorrect Pick: 0 Points
  • Lock of the Week (correct): 5 Points

Making Picks

  • Pick up to 7 games (either against the spread and/or over/unders).
  • Optionally designate one pick as your "Lock of the Week" for a bonus if it wins.
  • Picks must be submitted before each game's scheduled kickoff time.
  • Highest score at the end of each week wins a prize, and highest score at the end of the season wins the championship!
